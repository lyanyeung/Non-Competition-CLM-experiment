2008 with `HumanIndexMod.F90:1019`, calculated bare-ground 2 m RH = −0.05722%.
The actual forcing has RH = 0.023877% and QBOT = 7.02436e−8 kg/kg at
2008-01-11 15:15 UTC (local interval start 06:00). Source RH is missing;
QC2 temperature/VPD/pressure equal the same-release processed ERA companion.

and T about −38 C during the event, compared with forcing T about −32 C.

The operator approved this rule for all 2007–2025 US-ICh forcing records:

- Existing RH < 1%, independent CDS water RH >= 50%, and absolute air-temperature
  difference <= 8 C (1e−10 C comparison tolerance only).
- Preserve current TA, pressure, wind, radiation, precipitation, height, soil,
  PFT and parameter files. Recompute consistent water RH, VPD and specific
  humidity at the retained temperature and pressure. Preserve every other row.
- The source NetCDF has no VPD variable; VPD is recorded in the change ledger.
  Existing RH, QBOT and SHUM are updated; an existing VPD field would also be
  updated after its units are checked. No new met variable is added.
- Report RH<1 records outside the rule as RETAIN_OUTSIDE_RULE. They remain
  unchanged; the known failing timestamp must be eligible before any retry.

submission is reported without creating another job. Case-change failures or
uncertain submission attempts stop for inspection; they are never repeated
automatically. SIGKILL may leave active.lock, which must be inspected before
removal. Do not delete the state/backup to force another submission.
